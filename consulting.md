I take on freelance mobile development projects. That usually looks like one of the following:

1. **Starting zero to one**
   Taking an app from nothing to shipped — UI/UX design, prototyping, architecture, tooling, CI, and the first release to the App Store and Play Store.
2. **Building specific features**
   Dropping into an existing codebase and shipping real work — new screens, new flows, integrations with your backend — without a long ramp-up.
3. **Modernizing and improving performance**
   Swift and SwiftUI, Kotlin and Compose, moving off deprecated frameworks — plus the crashes, slow screens, flaky builds, and release cycles that have started to hurt.
4. **Enhancing your AI productivity**
   Getting real leverage out of AI tools — agentic workflows that fit how your team already works, the project conventions and guardrails that keep your team moving, and an honest read on where these tools help and where they might be slowing you down.
